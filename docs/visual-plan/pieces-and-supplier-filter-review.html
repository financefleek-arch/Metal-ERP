Pieces on the order page, and finding a supplier's items

Decided: a loose piece costs pack price ÷ pieces by default, with an optional premium % (on the link, and also when you create a catalog PDF). Whether pieces can be ordered is chosen per catalog link. Mock data and wording, not final.

1. The customer's catalog page

Every row now says how many pieces are in a pack, like the PDF does ("for 6 pcs"). When the link allows pieces, a row with a pack size gets a Pack | Piece switch next to the stepper. Items sold singly look as they do today. How many items per row (default 2), and whether the code and the pack line show, are set per link (section 2).
