3. WhatsApp templates to submit now (utility)

Approval takes days, so submit these first. Wording is transactional on purpose: promotional words would get them re-classified as marketing (dearer, capped).

order_receivedUtility
Hi {{1}}, {{2}} has received your order {{3}} ({{4}} items, ₹{{5}}). We will confirm availability shortly.
Button: View order (URL)
order_updateUtility
Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} is {{3}}. {{4}}
{{3}} = confirmed / invoiced / could not be accepted · {{4}} = invoice number and amount, or the shop’s reason
Button: View order (URL)
new_order_alertUtility

4. Questions for you

  1. Quantities: always whole packs (as shown), or should some items sell by the piece?
  2. Prices: the same price for everyone for now. OK, or do some customers need their own rate soon?
  3. Minimum order and terms line: per link (as drawn), or one setting for the whole shop?
  4. Order number: ORD-0042 style, separate from invoice numbers. OK?
  5. GST: the customer page shows prices before GST with a terms line. Until GST billing is built, is that right?
