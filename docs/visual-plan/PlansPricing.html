Plans, pricing and upgrade

Mockup only. Today a newcomer sees no price anywhere in the app (the landing page says "Free to start"; locked features say "Not on your plan" with an "Ask Fleek" button; the add-user limit says "Change the plan in Fleek"). This adds one plans page and puts upgrade in front of people at the moments they want it. Prices and plans shown are the owner's ladder (2026-10-29, revised); the page reads the live values from Fleek, so you edit them in the Fleek Plan templates screen and nothing here is hard-coded. Red numbers = gaps listed at the bottom.

Still open

  1. Payment details up front? You said we can choose. My recommendation is yes (UPI AutoPay mandate, nothing charged for 3 days): a trial that needs coming back to pay converts worse.
  2. WhatsApp needs more than 3 days for some shops (number not on the Business app yet, or the Facebook business admin is unavailable). Rule I suggest: the clock starts at enablement, our team may extend a Growth trial by up to 4 days if the delay is on Meta's side or ours, not the owner's wish to wait. OK?
