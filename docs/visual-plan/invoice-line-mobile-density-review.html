Decided — one line open at a time. Expanding a line (tap its collapsed row, or + Add another item) collapses whichever line was open. A line with a blocking problem still forces itself open on a finalize attempt, and if two lines are broken the first one wins focus. Least scrolling, one thing to look at — right for a layman.
