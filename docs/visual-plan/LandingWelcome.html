What changes on the landing page
  • Headline sells the result ("get paid faster") instead of naming the category.
  • Invoice and WhatsApp bubble are drawn in code, so no photos to source and nothing to load.
  • Prices move up in colour: Growth gets a brass border and "Most shops"; the free trial gets its own green strip.
  • The button repeats in the hero, after the prices, at the end, and as a sticky bar on a phone.
  • The "Tally Companion" name in the header and "Metal ERP" on sign-in need one name. Your call.
  • Add a description and a share image to index.html so a WhatsApp link shows a preview.
  • Numbers in the trust strip must be true. "2 min" and "₹0" are; do not add shop counts until we have them.

Questions for you

  1. Name: "Tally Companion" (landing, Play Store) or "Metal ERP" (sign-in)? One name everywhere.
  2. Sign-in with no account: say so plainly (better for people, but reveals whether a number is registered), or keep the same answer for every number and add "New here? Create an account" under the error?
  3. Second route when WhatsApp fails: SMS (cost, provider), call-back (uses Follow-ups, no cost), or both?
  4. Shorter sign-up (2D): do it now with the API change, or keep the current form and fix only the screens around it?
  5. Trust strip: keep the three claims shown, or wait until we have a real shop count and a testimonial to show?
  6. Day-1 nudge: approve a WhatsApp template for it, or email only to start?
