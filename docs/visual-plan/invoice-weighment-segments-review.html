Weighment segments on the sales invoice

Four changes to the invoice editor + printed bill. The big one — a running weight/count readout plus operator-drawn weighment segments that map onto the shop's platform-scale tickets — is sketched first. Three smaller fixes follow.

BUILT 2026-09-02 (uncommitted). Backend: migration 0011 (invoice_line.segment_no, invoice.weighment_slips JSON), domain/weighment.py, measure_for() on every read, finalize auto-closes open segments, PDF template gains the weighment rows + the weight/count box. Frontend: lib/weighment.ts mirror, running bar + Next segment + record-weight dialog + slip dividers, weighment block in the totals rail, discount toggles default to %, per-kg new item lands kg in the Unit field. 286 backend tests pass (+7 new); tsc + eslint + vite build clean. Decisions taken on the four open questions are noted inline below.

1 · Running weight & count, and weighment segments new

The operator loads goods onto the big platform scale as they add lines. A live bar under the line table shows accumulated weight and piece count. When the batch on the scale nears its ceiling, the operator reads the scale, taps Next segment, clears the scale, and keeps going. Each segment = a contiguous run of lines = one physical weighment slip. The bill prints them back as “Weighment 1: lines 1–6, 487.50 kg”.

Open questions for feature 1

  1. Confirm sheet on “Next segment” — keep the editable “weight shown on the scale” prompt (2nd mockup), or make the button cut the boundary silently and let the recorded weight just equal the line-weight sum?
  2. Editing after a segment is closed — if the operator inserts / deletes a line above a boundary, do the segment ranges auto-reflow, or does the segment lock its line set at close time?
  3. Non-weight lines inside a weight bill — a “nos” line (e.g. 2 gates) between weight lines: does it sit inside whatever segment it falls in (contributing to count, not kg), or are segments weight-only and piece lines float outside them?
  4. Storage — reuse the dormant invoice_line.weighment_id column + a new invoice_weighment table (seg no, recorded_kg, line range), or keep it lighter as a segment_no int on the line + recorded weights on the invoice header JSON?

2 · Weight & count always on the bill new

Independent of segments: every invoice — weight goods or not — shows a total weight and a piece count, on screen (totals rail, shown above) and on the PDF (the boxed line under amount-in-words, above). Count = sum of quantity on piece/nos lines (6 kadai + 12 tasla → 18 pcs); kg lines feed weight only. A pure-piece bill shows 0.00 kg weight; a pure-weight bill shows 0 pcs.

3 · Discount defaults to % change

Today a new line's discount toggle starts on ₹ (absolute). Flip the default to % for new rows and for the invoice-level discount (which today is a bare ₹ field on the rail — it gains the same ₹/% toggle). A reloaded saved line still shows ₹: the backend only ever stores an absolute amount, so a reloaded line can't know it was typed as a percent — the stored ₹ is exact and shown as-is.

4 · “Per kg” new item → Unit shows kg fix

When a free-typed item isn't in the catalogue, a strip offers per piece / per kg. Choosing per kg already sets uom:"kg" in code — this is a visible-behaviour check that the Unit dropdown actually lands on “kg” (not stays blank or snaps back to “nos”), and that the Rate label follows to ₹/kg.

Behaviour notes / scope

  • tax.py untouched. Weight and count are display aggregates over the existing quantity / uom columns — no change to line-total or grand-total arithmetic, no change to tax_vectors.json.
  • What is a “weight unit” for the total-weight sum: uom in {kg, kgs, g→÷1000, quintal→×100, ton/mt→×1000}. Everything else is a piece unit and feeds count. Final list to confirm.
  • Count = Σ quantity over piece-unit lines, shown as an integer (rounded if someone typed 2.5 nos). Line count is not what's shown.
  • Segments are entry-time only for totals purposes — the grand weight is the sum of all weight lines regardless of how they're split. Segment recorded-weights are a parallel record printed on the slip lines; they don't feed the money or the total-weight figure (which stays line-derived) unless we decide otherwise (open Q1/Q3).
  • Draft with open segment. A draft can be saved mid-weighment; the last segment stays open (no recorded weight) until closed or until finalize auto-closes it at the line sum.
  • PDF template. invoice_v1_nongst.html gains: full-width banded weighment rows in the line loop, and the weight/count/N-weighments box under amount-in-words. The React live preview mirrors both.
  • Migration. Depends on Q4 — either a new invoice_weighment table (0011) or a single segment_no column on invoice_line plus a JSON on the header. No other schema change.
