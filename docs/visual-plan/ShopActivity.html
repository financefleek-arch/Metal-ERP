Part 2. Metal ERP: a welcome that leads to the first invoice

The goal line is "first invoice sent", not "six steps". Customer and item are created inside the invoice, not before it. Firm address and bank are asked when they are actually needed, just before the invoice is shared.

2A. Welcome replaces the one-time page 17
