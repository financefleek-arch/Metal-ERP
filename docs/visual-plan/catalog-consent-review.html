Price-list consent: visual review

Two small screens, both feeding the consent record already built on the backend (migration 0054, PUT /api/parties/{id}/catalog-consent). Nothing here sends a catalog yet: this only records who has agreed to receive price lists on WhatsApp. Please say which of these you want changed before I code them.

1. Customer order page: a second, separate checkbox

Today the order form has one box, ticked by default: "Send me order updates on WhatsApp". That covers messages about this order only. Price lists are marketing messages, so they get their own box, unticked by default (Meta wants a clear, active opt-in; a pre-ticked box is the weakest kind).

Unticked every time. We still remember their name and phone for next time, but not this choice: a repeat visitor has to tick it themselves.

Wording names the shop (Meta's rule) and says what they will get and how to stop.

The order does not wait on it. Ticking or not changes nothing about placing the order.

2. Shop side: where that tick lands on the customer

An order only carries a name and phone. It is linked to a customer record when you review it. So the tick is kept on the order, and applied to the customer at that point, using the same "link or create customer" step you already do.

Order review (existing screen), one new line

Order ORD-0042 · Ravi Kumar · +91 98765 43210
Customer matched by phone: Ravi Traders.
Agreed to price lists on the order page · will be recorded when you confirm the customer
Use this customerChoose another

Only applied when you confirm the customer, never on a guess. A wrong phone match then cannot opt the wrong person in.

If they did not tick, nothing is shown and nothing changes. An existing "opted in" or "opted out" is never overwritten by an unticked box.

An opt-out always wins over a tick from an old order: if they replied STOP later, a stale order does not bring them back.

3. Party screen: record a verbal yes (or a no)

For customers who tell you at the counter or on the phone. One new block on the party form, under the contact fields. Counter staff can use it, because that is who hears the answer.
