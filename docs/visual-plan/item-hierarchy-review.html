Categories, product groups & sub-items

Three levels. A category is the coarse bucket a shop owns (steel · aluminium · iron · utensils). A product group is a family that shares an HSN / UOM / rate basis (MS TMT Bar, SS Balti). An item is the leaf you actually bill (MS TMT Bar 12mm). An inward bill line often names the group; the sale line names the leaf. The mapping is item.group_id and group.category_id — structural, generic, not metal-specific.

What each level owns
  • Category — just a name, tenant-scoped. The anchor. Both product_group and a group-less item carry a category_id.
  • Product group — the shared attributes: hsn_code, uom, item_type, metal, shape, grade, default_gst_rate. A leaf inherits these unless it overrides. HSN typed once per family, not per size.
  • Item — size_text / size_pos / size_label, its own default_rate / price_min / price_max / last_rate. The only level with money and usage counts.
  • A loose item (no group) still works — it carries its own category + HSN + everything, exactly as today. Grouping is optional; you reach for it when a family has sizes.

Inward names the group · the sale names the leaf

Inward bill line → resolve

  1. normalize("MS TMT Bar 12mm") exact-matches a leaf → done (specific bill).
  2. No leaf. normalize("MS TMT Bar") matches a product_group → link the line to the group. On approve: if the size is on the line (a dimension token), pick / create that leaf; else the reviewer chooses, or the line stays group-level (mixed sizes billed by weight).
  3. Fuzzy over leaves + groups + aliases. A group hit is shown as → MS TMT Bar (group) in the review cell with a size picker.
  4. No hit → stage a new item under the best-guess category, no group.
New step vs today: the ladder now also checks product_group.name_normalized. Purchase rate lands on the group's leaves (or the group's last_purchase_rate if line is group-level).

Sales invoice line → resolve

  1. Type-ahead searches leaves first (that's what you bill).
  2. If the top hit is a group, the combobox expands it inline — MS TMT Bar › [12mm] [16mm] [20mm] [25mm] — pick a size.
  3. Free-typed name, no hit → stays as text; the leaf is created at finalize (source=auto_from_invoice), no group unless the name clearly matches one.
A sale line's item_id is always a leaf. A group is never billed directly — it resolves to one of its sizes.

Why this generalises

Nothing above says "metal". The three tables are generic:
  • Utensils — category utensils → group SS Balti → leaves No.1 … No.5. Same model, size_label = the number.
  • Fittings — category fittings → group GI Elbow → leaves by bore 1/2" … 2".
  • A one-off — a loose item with a category_id and no group. Still fully billable.
  • A new business line — add a category, add groups under it. No schema change, no code change.
The "mapping" a shop tunes over time is two things: item.group_id (structural — done in the Items page or by the Tally importer from Stock Groups) and item_alias rows (learned — "this supplier's wording means this leaf/group", written on inward approve).
