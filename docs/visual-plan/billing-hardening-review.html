2. Firm page: "Payment reminders" setting (owner and accountant)

Payment reminders

Each morning the system lists customers whose bills are overdue. You approve the list; nothing is sent on its own.
Find overdue bills every morning
7, 15, 30
A bill is overdue after its credit days (currently 30, set on the Collections page). So the first reminder goes on day 37.
◉ I review the list first (recommended)
○ Send automatically, no approval
With automatic sending, a reminder goes out on its own, between 9am and 7pm, as soon as a bill reaches one of the days above. Bills that were already overdue for longer are not sent automatically; they wait on your list, so switching this on never messages everyone at once. At most 40 a day.
